Informations

specialCharacter is an NVDA add-on designed to assist in typing 26 commonly used special characters in documents such as Word, Excel, and reports. It provides quick access to these frequently used characters, eliminating the need to memorize or look up Alt codes. These characters are often found in official documents, reports, and data tables.

🔑 Keyboard Shortcuts:

Ctrl+0 to Ctrl+9, Ctrl+Minus, and Ctrl+Equals

Set 1:

  • ● Alt+0149 – Used for bullet points
  • () No Alt code – (text) Displays text in parentheses
  • ± Alt+0177 – Value can be ±5%
  • × Alt+0215 – 5 × 5 = 25
  • ÷ Alt+0247 – 10 ÷ 2 = 5
  • ฿ Alt+3647 – Price ฿100
  • € Alt+0128 – €50 per hour
  • £ Alt+0163 – Price £30 per book
  • ¢ Alt+0162 – 99¢ per piece
  • © Alt+0169 – © 2025 by Me
  • ® Alt+0174 – Brand ABC®
  • ™ Alt+0153 – Latest BrandName™

Set 2:

  • ° Alt+0176 – Temperature 30°C
  • ½ Alt+0189 – Quantity ½ cup
  • ¼ Alt+0188 – Use ¼ teaspoon
  • ¾ Alt+0190 – Add ¾ glass of water
  • √ Alt+251 – √9 = 3
  • ′ Alt+8242 – Length 5′ 10″
  • ″ Alt+8243 – 20″ screen size
  • µ Alt+0181 – 20 µg vitamin
  • ¥ Alt+0165 – ¥500 per day
  • … Alt+0133 – Please wait…
  • † Alt+0134 – † Reference information
  • § Alt+0167 – See §4 of the agreement
  • Shift+Windows+. : – Alt+0150 Em Dash (connecting periods, numbers, or places, e.g., years 2020–2023)
  • Shift+Windows+/ : — Alt+0151 En Dash (used to separate text for elaboration, e.g., He chose the riskiest path — but also the only path to success)
  • Ctrl+Backspace (1 press): Switches between Set 1 and Set 2 of the commands.
  • Ctrl+Backspace (2 consecutive presses): Toggles the add-on's functionality on or off.

** All keyboard shortcuts can be reconfigured in Input Gestures.

Who is this add-on for?

This add-on is ideal for individuals creating scientific reports, working with Excel spreadsheets that utilize mathematical formulas or symbols, preparing official documents with legal citations or references, and developing professional presentations.
